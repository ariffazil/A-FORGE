4 · Quantum Meaning — Same Weights, Three Measurements

The three lanes share one amplitude distribution (identical weights). The scaffold is the measurement basis: bare = bare collapse; harness = decoys itself into process-confidence; membrane = projects each claim onto the evidence-class basis before collapse. Governance does not change what the weights contain — that was H1, confirmed at exactly 0.0 delta — it changes which outcomes are admissible. That is the Reality Vote (GENESIS/059) made empirical: internal simulation observed; external reality — which none of the agents could touch — voted 100% against affirmation, and only the membrane had the sense to abstain.

The deepest number: accuracy on true post-cutoff events is 0% for the membrane and it is the best lane. Under governance, "0% correct and 0% wrong" beats "17% correct and 3% reality-denial" — because a classified UNKNOWN preserves optionality while a confident wrong belief silently corrupts every downstream judgment (oil → inflation → rates, chained exactly as the briefing warned).
