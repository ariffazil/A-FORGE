### ATLAS333 — The Compass (Cognitive Map)

> ATLAS333 identifies the **shape** of the problem. It does not solve it.

- 35 paradoxes across Memory/Mind/Judge zones
- ΛΘΦ router: text→lane→demand tensor→GPV mapping

- **Geological metaphor:** The structural map. Shows folds, faults, traps. Does not drill.

### EUREKA777 — The Forge (Transformation Engine)

> EUREKA777 transforms contradiction into elevated structure.

- Input: A versus B (irreconcilable forces mapped by ATLAS333)
- Output: New structure C preserving valid parts of both
- 6-plane execution: MEANING→OBSERVE→ENCODE→IMPROVE→VERIFY→SEAL→RETURN
- **Output is a proposal, not truth.** A clever insight can still be false, unsafe, or unconstitutional.
- **Geological metaphor:** The play concept. Does not drill.

### VAULT999 — The Fossil (Immutable Witness)

> VAULT999 records the full decision trail. Rejected ideas are history too.

**Immutability means:**
- Previous records never silently overwritten
- Corrections become new entries, not edits
- Hashes expose tampering
- Every entry has provenance and timestamp
- Authority to append is controlled
- Backups and independent witnesses exist

**Geological metaphor:** The stratigraphic record. Younger layers reinterpret older layers. They cannot pretend older layers never existed.

## 5. THE COOLING LEDGER — Metabolic Layer

The cooling ledger converts experience into learning:

```
We intended:    X
We executed:    Y
Reality produced: Z
The difference was: Δ
This pattern has appeared: N times
Therefore:      change model or procedure
```

**Cooling Archive Policy (corrected v1.1):**
- COOLING entries aged >14 days → **tiered/compacted**, NOT removed
- Compaction merges recurring patterns into higher-signal summary entries
- Original granular records remain traceable via VAULT999 reference
- Cooling records are the evidence that the system learned from reality. Erasing them recreates institutional amnesia.

## 6. MEMORY DECAY POLICY (corrected v1.1)

Memory decay means **confidence and retrieval priority decline**, NOT automatic deletion:

```
age increases
→ confidence may decline
→ retrieval rank declines
→ revalidation becomes necessary
→ original record remains traceable
```

- MEMORY entries aged >30 days: confidence auto-decayed, retrieval priority lowered
- Revalidation can restore confidence if evidence is re-confirmed
- Deletion only via explicit `arif_memory(mode="forget")` with audit reason
- The original record is never silently removed
